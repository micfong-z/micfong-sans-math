# Changelog

## v0.10 (2026-11-20)

First release, built from Lete Sans Math v0.63, IBM Plex Sans 1.1.0 and IBM Plex Mono 2.5.0.

- Latin, Greek and Cyrillic letters, digits and the matching math alphabets come from IBM Plex Sans: 1461 glyphs in the regular font and 814 in the bold.
- The monospace alphabet and digits come from IBM Plex Mono.
- `g` is single-storey in every style. `a` and `α` keep Plex's default forms.
- Italic `θ` is Plex's closed form, slanted; `ϑ` is Plex Italic's looped one.
- ϕ ϵ ϶ ϴ Ϝ, which Plex lacks, are borrowed from the Plex letters with the same shape (ф є э Ө F).
- Top-accent positions, italic corrections, subscript kerns and upright kerning are recalculated for the new letters.
